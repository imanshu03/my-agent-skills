# Deslop eval

Answer each check with pass or fail. Fix every failure before you return the text.

## Content

1. Are all facts, meaning, terms, quotations, code and required formats unchanged?
2. Is the text free of new claims, examples, numbers, quotes and opinions?
3. Is each claim that needs a source either sourced or flagged to the user?

## Edit size

4. Is the amount of change proportional to the slop, with strong sentences left alone?
5. In the personal register, would the writer recognize the voice as their own?
## House style

6. In the technical register, does the text follow ASD-STE100: common words used consistently, procedural sentences of 20 words or fewer, descriptive sentences of 25 words or fewer, one instruction per sentence, active voice, articles kept, noun clusters of three words or fewer?
7. Does the text meet Zinsser's four principles: simplicity, brevity, clarity and humanity?
8. Is the text free of staccato pairs, antithesis reframes, negative parallelism, isocolon metaphor-pairs and backward-references?
9. Is each code comment one clear line that says what the code does and why?

## Patterns

10. Is every generic sentence made specific or cut (portability test)?
11. Are puffery, -ing analysis, weasel attribution, kickers and recap endings gone?
12. Are binary contrasts, negative listing, dramatic fragments and robotic rhythm gone?
13. Are throat-clearing, faux-insight setups, colon reveals and metadiscourse gone?
14. Are AI vocabulary, fancy "is", metaphor nouns, mannered prose, filler and empty adverbs replaced with plain words?
15. Is passive voice limited to cases where the actor is unknown or does not matter?
16. Are over-compressed notes written as whole sentences?
17. Is formatting free of decorative bold, emojis, title case, curly quotes, inline-header lists and needless bullets or headers?
18. Are em dashes absent from short copy, and at most one or two in long copy?

## Output

19. Detect: does each finding name the pattern, quote the line and give a short fix, with no rewrite, score or guess about who wrote it?
20. Edit: does the output contain the full text and a short **What changed** list, unless the caller asked for another format?
